Counting your chickens

Version 1.27.0 · 12 November 2026

  • Eggs count. A packet's size can be a count now: a carton of 12 eggs, a dozen, a 6-pack, or one onion at a time. Cook something with 3 eggs and the carton says "about 9 left", and the shopping list knows two cartons are plenty. A packet with no size is still never guessed at, and "2 celery stalks" is not 2 celery.
  • Dark all the way to the edges. Choosing Dark in Bentotachi darkens the phone's own bars at the top and bottom too, instead of leaving bright strips on older phones. Choosing Light on a dark phone gets you dark icons up top you can actually read.
  • BBC Food's categories. On Browse, BBC Food has its Categories button back: easy soups, quick dinners and the rest, with their recipes to tick and save.
  • Better with no signal. A recipe opened offline gets its heart and ratings back once you're connected again, and photos that can't show offline no longer leave empty spaces to tap.
  • Fixes from a tester's notes. The tab names fit at the biggest phone text size. An export tells you the name it was really saved under. Adding a site that's already on your list says so. Picking the wrong file to restore says so in plain words, and picking a backup in Import & export tells you where to restore it instead. Thanks to a tester for the notes.

Nobody gets eaten

Version 1.26.0 · 12 November 2026

  • Your backup always lets you back in. Restoring a "Bento on this phone" backup on a new phone, or after Delete everything, used to ask for a password you'd never seen. Now it just opens. Thanks to a tester for a very thorough bug report.
  • Photos with no signal. Turn on Keep photos for offline in Settings and your phone keeps the recipes' photos (step photos too) and the pantry's, so they show in the no-signal corner like everything else. It says how much room it takes first; turn it off and the room's yours again.
  • Categories, tidied up. A category's name shows once and stays at the top of the screen while you scroll its recipes, so you always know where you are. On Browse, the Categories button sits neatly inside each site's row. Thanks to a tester for the notes.
  • Nobody gets eaten. A recipe for "6 people" now says "Serves 6" instead of "Makes 6 people". Cookies still get made: "Makes 12 cookies".
  • A tub is an amount. Running out of ice cream with "1 tub ice cream" already on the shopping list uses that line instead of adding a second. Same for punnets, bunches, dozens, cartons and sachets.
  • And the rest of that report. Delete everything really starts fresh. A paused timer says paused. The login screen says what's missing. A link without "https://" works. "Check for updates" says it checked. The first barcode scan is ready, and a saved backup tells you its size. Undo after renaming a pantry place keeps "Edit places" open, and a link to a page that's gone says so.

Dinner in the dead zone

Version 1.25.0 · 11 November 2026

  • Browse a site by category, minus the ads. On Browse, tap a site's Categories to see its categories as a plain list, read from the site's own menu. Open one, tick the recipes you want and add them all at once. Works for RecipeTin Eats, BBC Good Food and Just One Cookbook, and often for sites you add yourself. It was a tester's idea.
  • Works in the no-signal corner. Besides recipes and the shopping list, your phone now keeps the plan, the pantry, the fridge door and your shelves, so you can still check them when the signal drops. They're for reading until you're back: the shopping list is still the one you can tick off offline.
  • No more staring at "Loading…". If your server doesn't answer within 8 seconds, Bentotachi shows what's saved on your phone instead of waiting for minutes, and catches up by itself once the server's back.
  • A little cloud that only shows up when something's off. Beside the ⋮ menu, it says when Bentotachi can't reach your server (and when it last could), or that shopping changes are waiting to be sent. Tap it for Try again, and a reminder to check Tailscale if your server goes through it.

Older phones welcome

Version 1.24.0 · 11 November 2026

  • Bentotachi on older phones. "Bento on this phone" now works on older 32-bit phones too, so they can keep their recipes without a server. It was a tester's idea.
  • No more two milks. Running out of milk when "300ml milk" from a recipe is already on the list says so and uses that line, instead of adding a second. Milk chocolate doesn't count, however much you wish it did.
  • The recipe list keeps up. A recipe, rating or shelf someone else adds shows straight away, and live updates come back by themselves after the signal drops.
  • Steadier in a shop with bad signal. Something added just as the signal drops isn't added twice, and ticking it off meanwhile still counts. A café Wi-Fi's sign-in page can't pretend to be your server any more: your lists stay put and what you tick waits until you're properly online.
  • Amounts like "1 and 1/2 cups" scale and add to the list as one and a half. Step timers under different headings ("Sauce", "Pasta") no longer get mixed up.
  • Recipes saved from websites keep smaller photos, so they don't fill your phone. Recipes moved to another Bentotachi as a Paprika file keep their extra times and "Day 1" headings.
  • On a phone on its own, a backup that won't open no longer deletes your last one, and Bentotachi stops quietly keeping extra backups that took up space.
  • Small fixes: Edit places stays open after its questions, a place can't be called "Other", and a half-written fridge note or shelf tag isn't lost.

Every jar its own date

Version 1.23.0 · 10 November 2026

  • Each one in stock has its own dates. Every jar, tin and bag you count now has its own use-by, opened and bought date: tap one to change them. The item's use-by follows whichever goes off first, so you finally know which of the four Nutella jars to open. One without a use-by says how long ago it was bought instead.
  • The brands and sizes you buy. Each pantry item remembers the brands and sizes you've had, with their barcodes and store links, and a ★ on your favourite (what "+ 1 more" and the shopping list add). Another one you've had before is one tap away.
  • Pantry places. Tap the Pantry title to see just the fridge, the freezer or the chest freezer outside, or everything. Edit places adds, renames, deletes and drags them into order, for the whole household. Searching still looks everywhere.
  • Scanning in one go. Scan a barcode Bentotachi knows and it's counted by itself, with Undo in case you were only checking. Scanning something new counts the one in your hand too. If you date things, the message also offers "+ Use-by".
  • A new calendar for every date. The date big at the top with a pencil to type it instead, then the month with a list of years, then the days.
  • A tidier pantry item. Its photo sits beside its name, and the rest is sorted into Storage, Products we buy and In stock. The quick card shows what's in stock and what to add next.
  • Short messages after a tap ("Moved to the Fridge", "Already on the list") now show at the bottom like Undo, and go by themselves.
  • Using something up and buying more no longer keeps the old use-by date. The pantry says "+ 1 more" and "+ Add one" instead of "packet", since tins and bottles count too. The "once opened, keep in" place is gone: each jar says whether it's opened.

Your way, light or dark

Version 1.22.0 · 9 November 2026

  • Light or dark, your choice. Settings now has Appearance: leave it on Auto to follow your phone, or pick Light or Dark for Bentotachi alone. Each phone keeps its own choice.
  • A calmer dinner chooser. Recipes come first, with search and filters in one tray. A quick note ("Take-away", "Leftovers") sits at the bottom where it's always in reach, and "Out of" and "Planned" no longer shout in red. Tap Surprise me again to put a pick away.
  • A tidier profile. Tap your picture to change it, or the little ✕ to take it off. Your name and colour share one box, and Save sits right beside what you typed.
  • The household's country, tablespoon and meals on the plan moved to Settings, so the Household page is just who's in it and adding someone.

Everything in its compartment

Version 1.21.0 · 9 November 2026

  • A fresher, snugger look. Smaller text and spacing, so a whole shopping list or a week and a bit of the plan fits on one screen. Search boxes, shelves, pantry places, shopping aisles and the plan now sit in bento trays, each thing in its own compartment. Headings get Bentotachi's own font, pantry levels are little jars, and ticked shopping gets a brush stroke through it.
  • The plan, tidied. Each dish is a small chip with its picture. To take one off, drag it to the bin that appears at the bottom (with Undo, in case). A quiet + beside a meal adds a side, and the top says which days you're looking at, like "8 – 21 November".
  • Surprise me, now a little gacha. Each roll says something different, rare lines turn up now and then, and keep rolling long enough and Ben has opinions.
  • A proper fridge door. Notes sit on a jade door, each on paper in its writer's colour and held up by a magnet (mostly plums, sometimes a tiny Ben). Old notes fade and curl so you notice them, and Done peels a note off.
  • A shorter ⋮ menu. It now holds your profile, Keep screen on and a new Settings page for everything you set once. Your household and Log out moved to your profile.
  • Any colour you like for your name: the rainbow circle on your profile opens your phone's own colour picker.
  • Removing a pantry item or a saved site happens straight away with an Undo, and the questions that remain ("Delete this recipe?") are Bentotachi's own instead of the browser's grey box.
  • Adding to the pantry starts short (just the name and where it lives), tips behind a ? open as a small pop-up, and the shopping list's offline note says how many changes are waiting to sync.
  • Fixes: risoni isn't filed under Rice, a public Wi-Fi's sign-in page can't sign you out, and two quick taps on a recipe page no longer undo each other.

Linguine is not penne

Version 1.20.0 · 8 November 2026

  • Buy only what you're short of. If you count packets in the pantry, adding a recipe to the shopping list takes off what's in stock: 200 g of pasta in, 1.5 kg needed, and the list asks for 1.3 kg. Things you already have enough of start unticked and say so. Something you've marked Low still starts ticked, in case that bag is nearly empty.
  • Finished cooking? A ✓ Finished button sits at the end of every recipe. It marks the meal as cooked and asks Used anything up?, with what it worked out already ticked: one tin of chickpeas, 150 g from the open bag of linguine (which now remembers "about 50 g left"). Only if you count packets; otherwise it just marks the meal as cooked.
  • Kinds of one thing. Tell the pantry that Linguine, Penne and Spaghetti are each a kind of Pasta, and they sit together. A recipe asking for linguine only counts linguine (penne won't do, says the recipe), while one that just says "pasta" happily takes any of them. You can also mark a line Any pasta will do.
  • A friendlier pantry. Packets show as − 3 × 400 g +: tap − when one's used up, + for another. Tapping an item opens a few quick buttons (one more packet, used one, low, out) instead of the whole form. Ticking something bought on the shopping list adds its packet to the pantry by itself.
  • Add a whole category at once. Paste a recipe site's category page, or save one in Bentotachi's browser, and tick the recipes you want. A tester's idea.
  • Recipes from websites keep their ingredient sections ("For the marinade"), and Re-import from the website in a recipe's ⋯ menu brings in the site's latest version without losing your own notes.
  • Amounts in the method, if you'd like them: "Add the butter (2 tbsp)". Off unless you switch it on in the ⋮ menu.
  • Tips tucked behind a small ?, American spelling (favorite, color) for households in the United States, and, on the Google Play version, update prompts from the update after this one.

Sides, please

Version 1.19.0 · 8 November 2026

  • More photos. A recipe can have up to 20 photos besides its cover: how yours turned out, or one for a step, shown right in that step. Swipe through them at the top, or tap one to see it full size. Switch on Bring step photos in the ⋮ menu and recipes saved from websites bring their step photos too.
  • Step timers ring with the phone locked. Start one, put the phone down, scroll something else: it still rings when it's done. The first timer asks if it may send notifications (and, once a day until you say yes, if it may ring on time).
  • More than one recipe per meal. Curry and rice, pasta and its sauce: tap + Dinner under a day to add another. The plan now starts at yesterday, so today is always near the top.
  • Shopping, smarter. A recipe's Add to shopping list now offers All, Missing from pantry or Choose. On the shopping list, + Add from a recipe finds a recipe and adds its ingredients without leaving the list. Items keep the recipe's own words ("12 oz gluten free linguine"), without the chopping instructions.
  • Recipes as small cards, big cards or a list, under the new View button with the sort order and shelves. Close all shelves sits beside the count. Recipes remember the servings you last scaled them to, and the Recipes tab takes you back to the recipe you were on.
  • Fixes from testers (thank you!): the barcode scanner shows up in an empty pantry, the Plan button's day picker fits on small phones, and recipe cards fill their space again. A recipe's nutrition now says it's the recipe's own numbers.

Onigiri for everyone

Version 1.18.0 · 7 November 2026

  • Ratings are onigiri now. Tap up to five on a recipe. Until someone at home rates it, its card shows the website's own rating in grey stars, and the recipe page says "★ 4.8 from 2,341 ratings on" the site.
  • Every recipe card says where it's from: the website, or "home recipe". Two recipes with the same name tell you which is which.
  • Ingredients · Method · Notes above the ingredients jumps between them, and remembers where you were: from step 5 to the ingredients and back lands on step 5. Ingredients and Method are tidy cards now, and each step's timers sit inside that step.
  • A ⋯ next to Plan and Edit: Share, Save as a Paprika file (to send one recipe to a friend), Print, Duplicate (a copy to make your own version) and Delete, which used to live at the bottom of the page.
  • Nutrition facts are kept from recipe websites and Paprika files. Switch on Show nutrition in the ⋮ menu to see them. It's off unless you want it, and only for you.
  • Paste a whole recipe (from a message, an email, or the caption under a video) and Bentotachi sorts it into ingredients and method for you to check.
  • Adding a link you've already saved now says "You already have this", with Open it or Add another copy, instead of quietly making a twin.
  • Moving from Paprika got a lot easier: big collections import on a phone without running out of memory, your ratings come too, recipes Paprika saved from a website get their full-size photo, and you can open a Paprika file straight from Files, email or a chat with Import into Bentotachi. Going the other way, Paprika now accepts Bentotachi's files, and you can export just one shelf or your favourites.
  • Recipes from sites that list "1 tablespoon" and "olive oil" as two separate ingredients come in as one line, like a normal recipe.
  • Fixes: the built-in browser no longer jumps to the Play Store by itself when an advert tries it; a backup saved while Android closed Bentotachi in the background still ends up where you picked; scaling says "1 cup", not "1 cups"; rewording an ingredient keeps its pantry link; and household settings no longer flash back to the defaults.

Breakfast is now a thing

  • Breakfast and lunch on the plan, if you want them (Household, then Meals on the plan). Dinner-only households carry on as before.

Hello, Bentotachi

Version 1.11.0 · 2 November 2026

  • Bento is now Bentotachi. Plain "Bento" was taken. Several times.
  • Meet Ben, the screaming onigiri on chopsticks. He's fine, actually. He turns up on empty screens, errors and backups, in the mood each one deserves.
  • Works on just your phone: no server and no account to set up, just your name. Save a backup whenever you like.
  • The shopping list works with no signal: tick things off and add them in the supermarket's dead zone, and they're sent when you're back.
  • Undo for taking things off the shopping list and the plan.
  • Export all your recipes, with photos, as a Paprika file that other recipe apps can open.
  • "Our tablespoon": 15 ml, or the Australian 20 ml. Recipes say when theirs is different.
  • Pantry: notes on items ("3 cans"), "once opened, keep in the fridge", and a double-check when the scanner misreads a barcode.
  • Delete my account in Profile (on your own phone, Delete everything).

Shelves, hints and a stopwatch

Version 1.9.0 · 31 October 2026

  • Recipes sit on shelves (Mains, Desserts, "things we make when we're sad"), in whatever order you drag them.
  • Tag suggestions when you add a recipe.
  • Time it: a stopwatch for how long a recipe really takes (not the "ready in 20 minutes" lie), which can save the real times to the recipe. We cooked this remembers the day.
  • Hints for the other side of the world: grams beside pounds, °C beside °F, cm beside inches, and "capsicum" beside "bell pepper".
  • Imports work on more recipe sites, including old blogs and ones that write the recipe as plain text.
  • Edit just one part of a recipe (the method, the notes) right on its page.
  • The recipe writer's tips get their own notes, kept apart from yours.
  • Double-tap a recipe's photo to move and zoom it, so the food is actually in the picture.
  • Leaving with unsaved changes asks first.
