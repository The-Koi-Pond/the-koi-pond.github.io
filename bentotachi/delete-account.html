Deleting your Bentotachi account and data

You don't have an account with us. Bentotachi has no sign-up with The Koi Pond, and we never see your information. Your Bentotachi “account” is just the name and password you chose when you set the app up, and it lives in one of two places: on your phone, or on a Bentotachi server that someone in your household runs. Google asks every app with a login to explain how to delete it, so here's how, with or without the app.

In the app

Open the ⋮ menu, then your name (Profile), then Delete my account. On a Bentotachi that's on your phone only, the same place says Delete everything, which deletes all of Bentotachi's data from the phone.

Without the app

If Bentotachi was on your phone only (“Just me, on this phone”): uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept, or keep the app and clear it in your phone's Settings → Apps → Bentotachi → Storage → Clear storage. If you saved Bentotachi backups somewhere (like Google Drive), delete those files too: they're the only other copies.

If you used a household's Bentotachi server: your account is on that server, so ask the person who runs it to delete it. They can do it in a minute: open the server's admin page (the server's address followed by /_/), log in, open users, click your name, then the ⋯ at the top right, then Delete.

What's deleted, and what stays

Deleting your account deletes your login, name, email address (if you gave one), profile picture and colour, and your ratings. Recipes, lists, notes and plans you added stay for the rest of your household, since they're shared; ask the person running the server, or delete them in the app first, if you'd like those gone too. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Need a hand?

Email splash@thekoipond.dev, or open an issue at github.com/The-Koi-Pond/bentotachi/issues (those are public), and we'll help you find where your account is and how to remove it. More about what Bentotachi stores is in the privacy policy.
