What happens next

  1. I message you on Discord when the test is ready to start.
  2. Google needs to let you in. For that it wants the email address of your Google account (the one your phone's Play Store uses). I'll ask for it privately on Discord. It never goes on this website.
  3. You get a link, tap "Become a tester", and install Bentotachi from Google Play like any other app.
  4. Keep it installed for 14 days. Google counts the days in a row, so even if you're busy, please don't uninstall it before then.
