What happens next

  1. Join the testers group, using the Google account your phone's Play Store uses. Being in the group is how Google Play lets you into the test. It sends no emails, and other testers can't see your address. The group page will say “You don't have permission to access this content”: that's normal, there's nothing to read in there. Tap Join group at the top. It's the tiny button next to the name, which Google apparently made as small as legally allowed.
  2. Become a tester. The test is open! Once you're in the group, open the test's page on Google Play and tap Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available, you're not in the group yet (or Google is still catching up: give it a few minutes).
  3. Install Bentotachi from Google Play like any other app. Until Google finishes checking the store page, it may be called “com.xel.bento (unreviewed)”. Same app, worse name.
  4. Keep it installed for 14 days. Google counts the days in a row, so even if you're busy, please don't uninstall it before then.
